# Support

## Get Help

Read the [User Guide](https://github.com/lunchbox-labs/compasslet/wiki/User-Guide) for
how Compasslet works on iPhone, with CarPlay and in the Live Activity.

To report a problem or ask a question,
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/lunchbox-labs/compasslet/issues/new).
Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that cause the problem. Don't
include your location or other personal details.

## Common Questions

**The direction seems wrong.** The phone compass points relative to magnetic north. Move
away from magnets, metal and electronics, and follow any calibration prompt.

**CarPlay shows no direction.** Allow While Using location access with Precise Location
on iPhone, then open Compasslet from the CarPlay launcher. CarPlay shows the direction
you are travelling, so it updates once the car is moving.

**The Live Activity doesn't appear.** It appears during a CarPlay drive, and iOS decides
when to show it. Make sure Live Activities are allowed for Compasslet in the Settings
app.

## Diagnostics

If you are asked for diagnostics, follow
[Collect Diagnostics for Support](https://github.com/lunchbox-labs/compasslet/wiki/User-Diagnostics).
Diagnostics contain timing events with dates and times, not your location. See the
[Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
